My Story

A CNM who recognized a gap
and decided to fill it.

I've spent my career in women's health, first as a Labor and Delivery RN in the Metro Detroit area, then as a Certified Nurse Midwife in full-scope practice. I love this work, and I continue to practice as a CNM alongside running The Next Chapter Clinic.

Over time I noticed something that I kept coming back to: women going through perimenopause and menopause had so many questions, so many symptoms, and so much to discuss, but a standard appointment simply doesn't leave enough time to cover it all. There's the history, the education, the treatment options, the lifestyle piece. You can't do any of it justice in 10 or 20 minutes.

That gap in time, and in dedicated menopause education, is what The Next Chapter Clinic is built to close. When menopause care is the only thing on the schedule, there's finally enough room to do it right. Longer visits, real conversations, and a care plan that's built around the whole picture, not just the presenting complaint.

I am currently a Menopause Society Certified Practitioner (MSCP) — the gold standard credential in menopause care, held by a small fraction of providers nationwide. Menopause is not a side note here. It is the entire focus of this practice.

Why This Practice Exists

The gap in women's healthcare is real. And it's fixable.

Menopause affects every woman, yet it remains one of the most undertreated conditions in medicine. Women are routinely told their symptoms are "normal," handed a pamphlet, and sent home. Meanwhile, effective, evidence-based treatments exist that most women never even hear about.

The Next Chapter Clinic exists because women deserve access to a provider who has made menopause their entire specialty, not a side note between OB and GYN appointments.

Longer visits. Real conversations. A care plan built around you. That's not a luxury, it's what good care looks like.

My Philosophy

How I approach your care.

01

Evidence-Based, Not Fear-Based

Hormone therapy has been misunderstood for decades. I practice using current evidence, not outdated fears, and I'll take the time to explain the real data so you can make an informed choice.

02

Your Symptoms Are Real

Hot flashes, brain fog, sleep disruption, mood changes, these are not imagined and they are not something you just have to endure. Every symptom you experience deserves to be taken seriously and addressed.

03

Individualized, Always

There is no one-size-fits-all approach to menopause care. Your history, your symptoms, your values, and your goals all shape your care plan. I treat the whole person, not just the hormone levels.
