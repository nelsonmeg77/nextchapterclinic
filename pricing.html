Good to Know

A few things worth knowing
before you book.

We don't bill insurance directly

We are a cash-pay practice. We don't submit claims to insurance on your behalf. However, we provide a superbill on request — an itemized receipt you can submit to your insurance company for potential reimbursement if you have out-of-network benefits.

HSA and FSA cards accepted

Our services are eligible for HSA and FSA payment. Simply use your card at checkout like a regular debit card. If your plan requires a receipt, we provide one with all required information.

GLP-1 medication costs are separate

If prescribed a GLP-1 medication, the medication cost is between you and your pharmacy — not included in your visit fee. With the Eli Lilly savings card, commercially insured patients often pay as little as $25/month. Cash-pay options start at $299/month through LillyDirect.

Cancellation policy

We ask for 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations may be subject to a fee. No-shows without notice will be charged the full visit fee.
